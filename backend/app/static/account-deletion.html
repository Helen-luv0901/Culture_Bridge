刪除 Culture Bridge 帳號

你可以隨時刪除帳號，所有資料會一併刪除，無法復原。

在 App 或網頁中刪除

  1. 登入 Culture Bridge。
  2. 前往「我的」。
  3. 在「帳號」區塊按「刪除帳號」，再按「確認」。

會刪除的資料

  • 帳號、語言設定，以及你填的暱稱與真實姓名
  • 你分享的所有經驗（含 AI 摘要與翻譯）、你的反應與收藏
  • 你的辦理進度、卡關與差異回報
  • 你的檢舉、封鎖名單、與 AI 助理的對話

若你是審查者，你對官方流程的審查紀錄會保留但改為不具名，以維持流程驗證的可追溯性。

無法登入時

請寄信給 Culture Bridge 團隊，說明你登入時使用的帳號，我們會在 30 天內刪除並回覆你。

Delete your Culture Bridge account

You can delete your account at any time. All your data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

In the app or on the web

  1. Sign in to Culture Bridge.
  2. Open "My".
  3. Under "Account", tap "Delete account", then "Confirm".

What is deleted

  • Your account, language setting, nickname and real name
  • Every experience you shared (with its AI summary and translations), your reactions and saved experiences
  • Your procedure progress, stuck and difference reports
  • Your reports, blocked authors and assistant conversations

If you were a reviewer, your reviews of official procedures are kept without your identity so the procedures stay verifiable.

If you can't sign in

Email the Culture Bridge team with the account you signed in with. We delete it and reply within 30 days.
