刪除 Culture Bridge 帳號

你可以隨時刪除帳號，所有資料會一併刪除，無法復原。

在 App 或網頁中刪除

  1. 登入 Culture Bridge。
  2. 前往「我的」，按右上角的「設定」。
  3. 在頁面最下方按「刪除帳號」，再按「確認」。

會刪除的資料

  • 帳號、語言設定，以及你填的暱稱、真實姓名與大頭貼
  • 你發表的所有貼文（含 AI 摘要與翻譯）及貼文下的留言
  • 你寫的所有留言與回覆
  • 你按的「我也是」、對貼文的反應，以及你收藏的貼文
  • 你的辦理進度、卡關與差異回報
  • 你的檢舉（含對 AI 回答的檢舉）、封鎖名單、與 AI 助理的對話

詳見隱私權政策與使用條款。

若你是審查者，你對官方流程的審查紀錄會保留但改為不具名，以維持流程驗證的可追溯性。

無法登入時

請寄信給 Culture Bridge 團隊，說明你登入時使用的帳號，我們會在 30 天內刪除並回覆你。

Delete your Culture Bridge account

You can delete your account at any time. All your data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

In the app or on the web

  1. Sign in to Culture Bridge.
  2. Open "My" and tap "Settings" at the top right.
  3. At the bottom of the page, tap "Delete account", then "Confirm".

What is deleted

  • Your account, language setting, nickname, real name and profile photo
  • Every post you published (with its AI summary and translations) and the comments under it
  • All your comments and replies
  • Your "same here" likes on comments, reactions to posts, and saved posts
  • Your procedure progress, stuck and difference reports
  • Your reports (including reports on AI answers), blocked authors and assistant conversations

See the privacy policy and terms of use.

If you were a reviewer, your reviews of official procedures are kept without your identity so the procedures stay verifiable.

If you can't sign in

Email the Culture Bridge team with the account you signed in with. We delete it and reply within 30 days.
