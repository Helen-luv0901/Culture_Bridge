Culture Bridge 兒童安全標準

Culture Bridge 是由學生自主開發、提供在台國際學生生活資訊與經驗分享的 App。我們對兒少性剝削與性虐待（CSAE）採取零容忍。

禁止的內容與行為

嚴禁在 Culture Bridge 發布、分享或索取任何兒少性虐待內容（CSAM），或任何以性剝削、誘騙、騷擾未成年人為目的的內容與行為。違反者的內容會被移除，帳號會被停權。

適用對象

本服務的對象是大專院校的國際學生，不是為兒童設計的服務。

如何檢舉

  • 在 App 中，每則貼文與留言都可以按「檢舉」，選擇「色情內容」或其他原因送出。
  • 也可以封鎖對方，之後就不會再看到他的內容。
  • 也可以寄信到 {{SUPPORT_EMAIL_ZH}}，說明內容所在的位置。

我們如何處理

  • 檢舉會進入管理後台，由管理員審查。
  • 涉及兒少性剝削或性虐待的內容會立即移除，發布者的帳號會被停權。
  • 我們會依法通報主管機關與執法單位，例如 iWIN 網路內容防護機構與警察機關，並配合調查。

Culture Bridge child safety standards

Culture Bridge is an independent student-built app with everyday information and shared experiences for international students in Taiwan. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Prohibited content and behaviour

Posting, sharing or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or any content or behaviour meant to sexually exploit, groom or harass minors, is strictly prohibited. Such content is removed and the account is suspended.

Who the service is for

The service is for international university students. It is not designed for children.

How to report

  • In the app, every post and comment has a "Report" option; choose "Sexual content" or another reason.
  • You can also block the author so you no longer see their content.
  • Or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL_EN}} and tell us where the content is.

What we do

  • Reports go to our moderation console and are reviewed by moderators.
  • Content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation is removed immediately and the author's account is suspended.
  • We report it to the relevant authorities and law enforcement as required by law, such as iWIN (Taiwan's Institute of Watch Internet Network) and the police, and cooperate with their investigations.
