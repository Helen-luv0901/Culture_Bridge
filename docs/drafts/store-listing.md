## 繁體中文

**應用程式名稱**：Culture Bridge 文化橋

**簡短說明**（80 字以內）：
在台國際生的生活筆記：官方流程一步步辦，看看其他學生怎麼經歷相似情境。

**完整說明**：

剛到台灣，要辦居留證、工作證、健保、銀行帳戶……每件事都不知道從哪裡開始？教授一句「再看看」，不確定是不是拒絕？

Culture Bridge 把「該怎麼辦」和「別人怎麼經歷」放在一起：

📋 官方流程，一步步辦完
・工作證、居留證、健保、開戶、在學證明等常見流程
・每張流程卡標示驗證狀態、最後確認日期、近期有多少學生成功完成
・開始辦理後可以勾選進度；卡住時一鍵回報哪一步、什麼原因

🌱 學生經驗，多一種理解
・依「生活適應」「文化與溝通」與身分（學位生、交換生、華語生）瀏覽
・匿名分享一句話就好，AI 幫你整理成情境與給下一位學生的提醒，你確認後才發布
・中英文切換，原文永遠保留

💬 AI 助理
・用中文或英文問辦理問題，回答附上依據與驗證狀態
・不確定的事會直接說，並建議你向學校確認

🔒 安心使用
・分享一律匿名，不顯示姓名、學校或帳號
・可檢舉不當內容、封鎖作者，也能隨時刪除帳號與所有資料

學生經驗是個人觀點，不是官方規定；重要事項請以學校國際處與主管機關公告為準。

## English

**App name**: Culture Bridge

**Short description** (80 characters max):
Taiwan life notebook for international students: procedures and real stories.

**Full description**:

New to Taiwan and facing an ARC, a work permit, health insurance and a bank account, not sure where to start? Your professor said "let's see" and you can't tell whether that was a no?

Culture Bridge puts "how to do it" next to "how others experienced it":

📋 Official procedures, step by step
• Work permit, ARC, health insurance, bank account, enrollment certificate and more
• Every card shows its verification status, when it was last checked and how many students recently completed it
• Tick off steps as you go; if you get stuck, report which step and why in one tap

🌱 Student experiences, more ways to understand
• Browse by life adaptation or culture and communication, and by degree, exchange or language student
• Share anonymously in a sentence; AI organizes it into the situation and a reminder for the next student, published only after you confirm
• Switch between Chinese and English, always with the original kept

💬 AI assistant
• Ask about procedures in Chinese or English; answers cite their basis and verification status
• When it doesn't know, it says so and suggests checking with your school

🔒 Safe to use
• Everything you share is anonymous: no name, school or account shown
• Report content, block authors, and delete your account and data at any time

Student experiences are personal views, not official rules. For anything important, follow your school's international office and official announcements.
