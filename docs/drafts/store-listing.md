## 繁體中文

**應用程式名稱**：Culture Bridge 文化橋

**簡短說明**（80 字以內）：
在台國際生的生活筆記：查政府機關回覆的官方問答，看看其他學生怎麼經歷相似情境。

**完整說明**：

剛到台灣，居留證快到期、想打工不知道要不要工作證、健保什麼時候能加保……每件事都不知道從哪裡查起？教授一句「再看看」，不確定是不是拒絕？

Culture Bridge 把「官方怎麼說」和「別人怎麼經歷」放在一起：

📋 官方問答，查得到依據
・收錄教育部「大專校院境外學生輔導人員支援體系計畫（NISA）」的常見問答，由內政部移民署、勞動部、教育部、僑務委員會、健保署等機關回覆
・依 16 個分類瀏覽：簽證/居留證、在學期間工讀、健保/保險、獎學金/助學金、畢業後留臺工作等
・可依身分（外國學生、僑生、港澳生、陸生）篩選，也能用關鍵字搜尋
・每一題標示回復機關、發布日期，並附上原文連結；官網有英文版的題目也提供英文
・題目下方列出其他同學分享的相關經驗

🌱 學生分享，多一種理解
・依生活、美食、住宿、交通、學業、文化等分類瀏覽同學的貼文
・寫下標題和內容就能發文，AI 幫你建議標籤並自動翻譯成中文或英文；原文永遠保留
・可以匿名，也可以用暱稱或真名

💬 AI 助理
・用中文或英文發問，助理會根據官方問答回答，並告訴你是哪個機關、什麼時候回覆的
・有同學分享過相關經驗時，會一併推薦給你
・官方問答沒有提到的事會直接說，並建議你向學校國際處或主管機關確認

🔒 安心使用
・預設匿名分享，要不要顯示暱稱或真名由你決定；學校與帳號永遠不公開
・可檢舉不當內容、封鎖作者，也能隨時刪除帳號與所有資料

學生分享是個人經驗，不是官方規定；規定可能變動，重要事項請以學校國際處與主管機關最新公告為準。

## English

**App name**: Culture Bridge

**Short description** (80 characters max):
Official Q&A for international students in Taiwan, plus real stories from peers.

**Full description**:

New to Taiwan, with an ARC about to expire, a part-time job that may need a work permit, and no idea when health insurance starts? Your professor said "let's see" and you can't tell whether that was a no?

Culture Bridge puts "what the government says" next to "how other students experienced it":

📋 Official Q&A, with the source
• The FAQ of the Ministry of Education's NISA program (Network for International Student Advisors), answered by agencies such as the National Immigration Agency, the Ministry of Labor, the Ministry of Education, the Overseas Community Affairs Council and the National Health Insurance Administration
• Browse 16 categories: Visa and ARC, Part-time Job and Work Permits, Health and Insurance, Scholarships, Staying in Taiwan for Employment and more
• Filter by identity (foreign, overseas Chinese, Hong Kong and Macao, or mainland Chinese students) or search by keyword
• Every question shows the answering agency, the date and a link to the original; questions the site publishes in English are shown in English
• Related posts from other students appear under each question

🌱 Student posts, more ways to understand
• Browse posts by life, food, housing, transport, study, culture and more
• Write a title and your story to post; AI suggests tags and translates it into Chinese or English, always keeping your original
• Post anonymously, or under your nickname or real name

💬 AI assistant
• Ask in Chinese or English; answers come from the official Q&A and say which agency answered and when
• When other students have shared something related, it recommends their posts too
• When the official Q&A doesn't cover something, it says so and suggests checking with your school's international office or the agency in charge

🔒 Safe to use
• Anonymous by default; you decide whether to show a nickname or your real name, and your school and account are never shown
• Report content, block authors, and delete your account and data at any time

Student posts are personal experiences, not official rules. Rules change: for anything important, follow your school's international office and the latest official announcements.
